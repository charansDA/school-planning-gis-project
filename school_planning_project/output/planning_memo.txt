School Planning Analysis Memo
================================

Project summary:
This prototype geocodes student points, assigns them to planning units and school attendance boundaries, and summarizes enrollment pressure for district planning.

Key findings:
1. Oakridge Elementary has 97 students against a capacity of 170 (57.1% utilization), making it the highest-pressure campus.
2. Northeast Redevelopment Area is the strongest growth area with an expected growth rate of 9.3% and currently feeds Oakridge Elementary.
3. This workflow creates reusable outputs for planning memos, dashboard tables, and school locator maintenance.

Recommended next steps:
- Validate addresses before geocoding to reduce assignment errors.
- Monitor redevelopment-heavy planning units each semester.
- Review whether attendance boundary adjustments or capacity changes are needed.
